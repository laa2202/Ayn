The problem isn't collecting data. It's knowing what to process.

Industrial drones generate extensive imagery, but flight time, onboard compute, operator effort, and repeat inspections limit how much useful information each mission delivers.

01 / SENSING

Limited flight time

Aircraft must budget energy for propulsion, sensors, compute, and a safe return.

02 / PROCESSING

Heavy inference

Running expensive AI on every frame may waste processing resources where little has changed.

03 / VALIDATION

Uncertain findings

Ambiguous locations and observations may require another viewing angle or repeat visit.

02

Built on stereo-infrared tracking research.

What we've implemented

An FPGA-based dual-camera infrared beacon tracker that reduces incoming camera streams to lightweight centroid measurements, estimates camera geometry, and triangulates live 3D positions.

View foundational research ↗

What comes next

Extend the idea from efficient tracking of cooperative infrared beacons to predictive sensing of industrial environments—using probabilistic models and selective AI processing. Passive thermal defect detection has not yet been demonstrated.

03

One intelligence engine. Two products.

AYN ONE / Hardware

A purpose-built industrial drone prototype with stereo/infrared sensing, onboard compute, and a custom PCB for power distribution and measurement.

AYN OS / Software

A separately licensable intelligence layer using Hidden Markov Models, Bayesian inference, quantitative optimization, and selective AI verification to allocate sensing and compute.

OUTPUT

04

The make-or-break test: lower cost per verified kilometer.

Our hypothesis: pairing compute-aware perception with measured power management can reduce the total cost of industrial infrastructure inspection without compromising detection reliability.

A custom power-distribution board does not itself establish battery-life savings. We must measure actual electrical losses and energy use against a commercial baseline.

Cost per verified km

Total mission cost ÷ kilometers inspected to an agreed quality standard.

Recall at fixed false-alarm rate

Maintain inspection quality while processing selectively.

Watt-hours + compute per km

Measure whole-aircraft energy, onboard compute, and repeat passes—not AI workload alone.

01 / Benchmark

Measure commercial drone energy, inference workload, and inspection performance.

02 / Prototype

Integrate predictive perception and custom power monitoring on the first aircraft.

03 / Validate

Run matched pipeline-inspection tests: same conditions, same quality requirements, total cost compared.

Validation plan: Compare continuous vs selective inference on the same Jetson, benchmark stock vs custom power electronics independently, then test the integrated aircraft. Measure detection recall, AI compute energy, total mission energy, flight endurance and operating cost per verified kilometer.

05

Four applications. One decision engine.

AYN models where important information may be, processes selectively, and verifies findings before acting. These are potential applications—not current deployments.

01 / SEARCH & RESCUE

Find a missing person.

Combine last-known position, terrain, movement assumptions, and incoming observations to prioritize search regions—with systematic checks outside predicted hotspots.

02 / WILDFIRE RESPONSE

Watch the shifting edge.

Estimate which sections of a California wildfire perimeter are changing or poorly observed, then prioritize authorized aerial rescans and hotspot verification.

03 / INDUSTRIAL INFRASTRUCTURE

Inspect pipelines intelligently.

Use route geometry, historical imagery, and cheap preliminary signals to reserve high-cost inference and repeat passes for uncertain or potentially anomalous segments.

04 / MARITIME & ENERGY

Understand disrupted corridors.

Fuse AIS vessel tracks and satellite observations to flag changing maritime activity near the Strait of Hormuz or Caspian energy routes. Drone verification is location- and authorization-dependent.

Illustrative model / search & rescue

Uncertainty guides the next look.

A Hidden Markov Model estimates the probability of a target occupying each zone as conditions and observations change. AYN schedules detailed vision processing where it offers high information value, while maintaining baseline coverage to avoid blind spots.

Concept visualization; probabilities and performance are not measured results.

06

Start with infrastructure. Build for autonomous sensing.

Our first proposed market is industrial pipeline inspection. If we can verify more infrastructure per flight and per dollar, the underlying intelligence software could extend to other drones and sensing systems.

Early-stage research thesis · Not yet a demonstrated cost advantage
